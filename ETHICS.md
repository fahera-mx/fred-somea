# FRED SOMEA - Responsible Use Policy

`FRED SOMEA` was created to support accountability, education, and research. The tool is powerful, and with that power comes responsibility. This document sets out the principles of ethical use that all users are expected to follow.

---

## Intended Use

FRED SOMEA is designed to:

- Preserve fragile digital content for future reference and accountability.  
- Support journalists, NGOs, and researchers in documenting human rights abuses, disinformation, and public-interest events.  
- Enable responsible network analysis of public social media interactions.  
- Advance education and open research into digital narratives.  

---

## Prohibited Use

The following uses are explicitly prohibited:

- Stalking, harassing, or targeting individuals.  
- Collecting, storing, or distributing **private or non-consensual data**.  
- Surveillance for oppressive, exploitative, or harmful purposes.  
- Commercial exploitation without a proper license from FAHERA CORE ENTERPRISE HOLDINGS S. DE R.L.  

---

## Ethical Guidelines

We encourage all users to:

- Respect the privacy and safety of individuals when handling public data.  
- Follow established **OSINT ethics frameworks** (e.g., Bellingcat, Citizen Lab).  
- Anonymize or redact sensitive information when sharing findings.  
- Maintain transparency in research methods and sources.  

---

## Enforcement

Use of FRED SOMEA in ways that violate these principles is strictly forbidden. Violations may result in revocation of access, refusal of commercial licensing, or legal action where applicable.
